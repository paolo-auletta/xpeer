# X-Peer landing surface brief

- Scope and mode: full landing page, designed to persuade Bocconi students to join the first cohort.
- Audience: first-year students seeking a near-peer guide and second-year students ready to mentor.
- Primary job: make the one-year-ahead mentorship model immediately understandable and credible.
- Primary action: choose either “apply as a first year” or “become a mentor.”
- Proof: specific situations students face, the four mentorship pathways, and the broader peer network around each mentor.
- Constraints: use the supplied Figtree family, X-Peer logo system, forest/ivory/lime palette, and one cropped style from each pathway illustration; application links remain inactive until the external forms are ready.
- Chosen direction: a photo-led editorial narrative. The hero labels two students one year apart and connects them with a single lime route; the community section lets real people carry the proof.
- Memorable moment: the first-year and mentor labels sit directly on the hero photograph, joined by one purposeful path rather than an abstract orbit.
- Intentionally unresolved: replacement photography featuring actual X-Peer members, social-profile URLs, analytics, and the two external application-form URLs.
